Stochastic Reaction Simulator

Write a reaction model, simulate it with the Gillespie algorithm, and see how much it varies from run to run. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

        Model grammar
        
One statement per line. Blank lines and comments (# or //) are ignored.

Reaction:   name: reactants -> products; rate_constant_name
            J1: A + B -> C; k1
            J2: 2 A -> ; k2         (a blank side is allowed)
            J3: -> A; k3            (production from nothing)
Values:     A = 100                 (species: whole number, 0 or more)
            k1 = 0.001              (rate constant: number, 0 or more)

* At most two reactant molecules per reaction (coefficients sum to 2 or less).
* Coefficients are positive whole numbers: 2 A.
* A $ prefix marks a boundary species ($S): it takes part in the rate
  but its amount never changes.
* Every species and every rate constant needs a value.
* Rates use mass action: rate constant x number of reactant combinations.
* Antimony text from this page's Export loads back in (e.g. J1: A + B -> C; k1*A*B).

    V1 · Steady-state spread
    
    
    Runs the model in the text box 1000 times and reports the mean and variance of each species’ final value. For a simple birth–death model the variance ÷ mean should be 1. PASS if it is within 10% of 1; species whose mean is 0 show “n/a”.
